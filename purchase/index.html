03 — FAQ

Payment and engagement terms

Can I pay by card?

Yes. Fixed-price offers can use a hosted secure checkout link. For scoped work, the secure payment link is issued after the final scope and price are approved.

Can my company pay by invoice or ACH?

Yes. Select invoice / ACH in the purchase form. The invoice will identify the service, scope, payment timing, and any recurring terms.

Why can’t every service be charged immediately?

Build sprints, retainers, content engines, evaluation harnesses, and asset licenses can vary by scope. Charging before the deliverables and terms are agreed would create the wrong incentives and a worse buying experience.

When does work begin?

After the required intake and access details are confirmed, the written scope or offer is accepted, and the applicable payment or deposit has cleared.

Need a custom route?

Describe the constraint before you purchase.

Use the existing service inquiry path when you need procurement paperwork, an NDA, a custom license, or a quote outside the listed packages.
