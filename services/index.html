Product and Systems Audit

A concentrated look at the system and the work around it, so the next decision is based on the real bottleneck rather than a hunch.

  • Architecture and workflow review of the system as it operates today
  • Bottleneck findings tied to the failure, delay, or cost that matters
  • Prioritized plan for the changes worth making first
  • Delivery estimate and walkthrough your team can use to decide what happens next

02 — Fit

A clear problem makes for useful work.

Good fit

There is a constraint you can point to.

  • Fragile manual workflows that need a reliable path
  • Expensive API behavior that needs evidence and control
  • Broken data flow between systems people depend on
  • One bounded product or internal-tool problem with an owner
Not a fit

The outcome needs more definition first.

  • Staff augmentation without a defined outcome
  • Speculative mass-content schemes
  • Projects requiring unsupported legal or regulated-domain guarantees
